About STILL

A ridge, a dome and nothing else to do

STILL is a small glamping retreat in the Ukrainian Carpathians. Glass domes stand on the edge of a forested ridge and face west, so every evening ends with the sun going down behind the mountains.

It is made for slowing down: no schedule and no noise, a warm bed under the open sky and a fire on your own deck.

Experiences

Days that move at the speed of the mountains

Nothing here is on a timetable. Do one thing a day, or nothing at all.

  • Fire pit evenings

    Blankets, tea and a fire on your deck as the valley goes dark.

  • Sunrise above the clouds

    Morning mist settles in the valley below the domes.

  • Hot tubs

    A steaming wood-fired tub on the deck, with the hills turning gold.

  • Forest trails

    Walk among firs and beech, then come back to a warm dome.

  • Stargazing

    Far from city lights, clear nights are full of stars.

  • Doing nothing

    A book, a blanket and a view that changes every hour.
